Human-centered Embodied Agent InteRaction Lab

HEAR Lab

人本具身交互实验室

We study how people and embodied agents — robots, virtual agents, and AI systems with a body — can understand each other and keep adapting to one another, so that collaboration is transparent, aligned, and trustworthy in real-world settings.
